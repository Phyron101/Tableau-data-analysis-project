# Tableau Dashboard: Bookstore Sales Analysis

## Overview
Interactive Tableau dashboard analysing bookstore sales by genre, region,
publisher and week.

## Tools
Tableau

## Dashboard Views
- Q1 Sales by Genre: stacked bars showing genre sales split by region
- Weekly Sales Trend: time analysis of sales by genre
- Regional Performance Overview: sales compared across West, East, Central, South
- Publisher Performance: publishers ranked by sales

## Interactivity
Filters for genre, region and publisher name.

## Key Findings
- West was the top-selling region
- History and Sci-Fi were the top-selling genres
- Weekly sales peaked around weeks 10 and 12
